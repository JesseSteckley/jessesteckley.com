# Jesse Steckley

Personal site for **Jesse Steckley (Aubdauban)** — Economic Reconciliation Advisor from Wasauksing First Nation (Robinson Huron Treaty 1850). Founder of [Waaseyak](https://jessesteckley.com). Based in Winnipeg, Treaty 1 Territory.

**Live:** [jessesteckley.com](https://jessesteckley.com)

Works with **Indigenous communities and allies** on the full range of economic reconciliation — strategy, people, capital, systems, and digital tools. Not a First Nations-only mandate, and not limited to workforce or procurement.

## What’s here

- Point-of-contact site (approach, about, impact, contact)
- Looping portrait hero, tobacco / charcoal brand
- Live GitHub contribution graph under Impact
- Structured data and share card for search / unfurl

## Stack

React, TypeScript, TanStack Start, Tailwind.
